Frequently Asked Questions

1. How does Dynamic UPI QR payment confirmation work?

Once you submit your form, a unique UPI QR code locked to the exact fee is generated. Pay using any UPI app — our system automatically detects payment confirmation within seconds without entering any UTR number.

2. Can I edit my documents after paying?

Yes! As long as your status is Pending, you can edit details or replace uploaded files from your Dashboard. Once our admin downloads your documents for processing, the status changes to Applied and editing is locked.

3. How do I track my official PAN / Document status?

When your status changes to Completed, your Dashboard and Track Status page will display your Official Application Number, Tracking Date of Birth, and direct official portal tracking link.
